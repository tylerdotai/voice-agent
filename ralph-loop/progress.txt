## 2026-05-09 - Story 4: Build Baseline Voice Loop
- **What was implemented:** Created baseline_loop.py with full voice pipeline
- **Files changed:** baseline_loop.py (8716 bytes)
- **Pipeline:** mic → VAD (energy-based) → Faster-Whisper STT → Ollama LLM (qwen2.5:0.5b) → Kokoro-ONNX TTS → speaker
- **Benchmark:** LLM response ~0.9s, TTS ~1.77s audio generated
- **Learnings:**
  - qwen2.5:0.5b gives 0.9s total response time for simple queries
  - Kokoro-ONNX generates audio in ~1.7s for 30-word response
  - Queue-based architecture with streaming LLM→TTS
